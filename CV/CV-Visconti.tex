\mediumtitle{Brief description}

Alessia Visconti is an expert in biostatistics and computational medicine. Her research deals with the development and application of statistical and computational methods to identify multi\emph{-omics} modifications influencing complex human phenotypes. 
She has also worked on the problem of knowledge discovery in biological data, developing new approaches to solve biological tasks, and on the reverse engineering of gene regulatory networks.
